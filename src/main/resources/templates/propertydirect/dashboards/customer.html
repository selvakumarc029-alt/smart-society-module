Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about visits, owner contacts, and home services.

How do I reschedule a site visit?

Go to the Site visits tab in your sidebar, locate your upcoming visit, and click 'Reschedule Slot'. The property owner will be notified instantly.

How fast do owners respond to contacts?

Verified owners typically respond within 15 to 30 minutes via WhatsApp or phone. You can view all responses under the Contacts tab.

Can I cancel a booked Home Service?

Yes, you can cancel or reschedule any home service (cleaning, carpentry, painting) free of charge up to 2 hours before the service slot.

Are properties verified before publishing?

Every listing on PropertyDirect undergoes automated RERA and owner identity verification before going live to ensure 100% zero brokerage transparency.
